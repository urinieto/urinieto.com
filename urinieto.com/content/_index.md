Hola! I'm **Uri Nieto**, a researcher in the Sound Design AI Group at [Adobe Research](https://research.adobe.com/). I build human-centered AI for audio-visual creators: tools that help people find, generate, and shape sound for their stories while keeping them in creative control. My work spans machine learning and signal processing for audio, from understanding to generation, including multimodal models that connect sound with text, images, and video. I'm one of the original authors of [librosa](https://librosa.org/), and I also [make music](/music-videos/), and often [scream](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1dAdsOjVChI).

I'm always looking for brilliant graduate students to collaborate with. If you're interested in an internship at Adobe Research, [get in touch](https://www.linkedin.com/in/urinieto/)!
