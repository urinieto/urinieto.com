Hola! I am Oriol (Uri) Nieto (he/they), a Senior Research Engineer II in the Sound Design AI Group at [Adobe Research][1], where I build human-centered AI for audio-visual creators. I was a Staff Scientist at Pandora before that, and I got my Ph.D. from NYU. I also [make music](/music-videos/).

{{< img src="/wp-content/uploads/2025/uriColorized.png" alt="" title="uri" width="350" class="" >}}

## Research

My research focuses on machine learning and signal processing for audio, from understanding to generation: multimodal models that connect sound with text, images, and video; diffusion models for conditional audio generation; contrastive and representation learning; and music information retrieval. Before Adobe, I worked on large-scale music recommendation at Pandora. I am one of the original authors of the audio processing Python library [librosa][2] and of the Music Structure Analysis Framework [msaf][3]. All my papers, including my Ph.D. dissertation, are on the [Publications][4] page.

## At Adobe

I love seeing research end up in the hands of creators. Some of the features I have helped ship:

- [**Generative Soundscape**](https://research.adobe.com/news/adobe-max-2026-adobe-research-puts-more-control-in-creators-hands-with-new-ai-tools/) in Premiere Pro, released at Adobe MAX 2026. Lead researcher.
- [**Generative Sound Effects**](https://research.adobe.com/news/adobe-researchers-pioneer-the-future-of-sound-design-with-gensfx/) in Firefly.
- [**Generative Extend**](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere-pro/using/generative-extend.html) in Premiere Pro. One of two lead researchers.
- **Semantic Audio Search** in [Adobe Stock](https://stock.adobe.com/) and [Premiere Pro](https://blog.adobe.com/en/publish/2025/11/19/adobe-premiere-adds-smarter-search-faster-edits-seamless-collaboration). Lead researcher.
- [**Audio Category Tagging**][5] in Premiere Pro. Lead researcher.

## Community

I have been an active member of the [ISMIR](https://ismir.net/) community since 2012, as an author, reviewer, meta-reviewer, and organizer. I was General Co-Chair of [ISMIR 2024](https://ismir2024.ismir.net/) in San Francisco. I am an Area Chair and reviewer for [ICASSP](https://2027.ieeeicassp.org/), and I regularly review for [TISMIR](https://transactions.ismir.net/), [TASLP](https://signalprocessingsociety.org/publications-resources/ieeeacm-transactions-audio-speech-and-language-processing), [CVPR](https://cvpr.thecvf.com/), and [ICLR](https://iclr.cc/).

I love mentoring. I have been a mentor in the [WiMIR Mentoring Program](https://ismir.net/wimir/) since it began in 2016, and I am also a mentor for [LatinX in AI](https://www.latinxinai.org/mentorship-program) and for the graduate student mentoring program of the [Music Technology Group](https://www.upf.edu/web/mtg) at Universitat Pompeu Fabra.

## Music

I sing, play guitar, violin, and drums for the psychedelic, depressive, humorous, transatlantic, and transcontinental band [La Bossa d'Urina](http://labossadurina.com), and play cajón and guitar and sing for the rumba band [Rumbahía](https://open.spotify.com/artist/33eHLBhvDL2U5Xaar4Quel?si=32VQzSBiQGiO98g_wj9D7Q). Before that, I was the singer, guitarist, and violinist of [Sargon](https://www.facebook.com/sargonmetal/) for 10 years and the singer of [Arkaen](https://open.spotify.com/artist/0JrPYalpR8Tnzln37ZQUSI?si=RmWGGL1tSUabK2XS1GBPxw). I created the [Growl Hero](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~urinieto/256/growlhero/index.html) and the [Ultimate Screaming Lessons](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1dAdsOjVChI&list=PL473A703483112034), and I direct **Jarl'em All Studios** in ~~Harlem, Brooklyn~~ Oakland, California. My [YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@enochroot83) has over 100 music videos.

## Earlier

At NYU, I taught [C Programming for Music Technology][6] and [Applications on iOS Platform](https://ccrma.stanford.edu/~urinieto/MARL/iOS/), and worked with [Jordan Rudess][7] of [Dream Theater][8] on the iOS apps [MorphWiz][9] and [SketchWiz][10]. Then Python took over my life.
